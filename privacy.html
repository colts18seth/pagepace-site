PagePace · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

PagePace (“we,” “us,” or “the app”) is a personal reading-pace planner. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

If you have questions, contact: lafolletteproductsllc@gmail.com

Summary

  • PagePace does not require an account.
  • Your reading goals, books, and progress logs are stored on your device.
  • We do not operate a PagePace server that stores your reading data.
  • If you use book search, your search query is sent to the Open Library public API to look up titles.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

When you use PagePace, the app may store the following locally on your device (for example, using on-device storage such as AsyncStorage):

  • Reading goal settings (such as target number of books, deadline, reading speed, and days off)
  • Books you add (title, author, page counts, series details, reading status, and progress)
  • Reading logs you enter (pages read and related progress)
  • App preferences related to setup and in-app guidance (such as whether you have completed onboarding or the tab tour)

This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it yourself (for example, by taking screenshots) or unless your device’s own backup features (such as iCloud Backup) copy app data as part of a device backup. Those backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by PagePace.

You can remove local app data by deleting the app or using any in-app reset option (if available).

Information sent over the network

Optional book catalog search

If you type in the book search field, PagePace may send your search text to Open Library (openlibrary.org) to fetch matching titles, authors, page counts, and related catalog metadata. This helps autofill book details. You can still add books manually without using search.

Open Library is a third-party service operated by others. Their handling of requests is governed by their own policies (see Open Library). PagePace does not control Open Library’s servers or logs.

We send a simple app identifier in the request user-agent (for example, that the request comes from PagePace) so the catalog provider can recognize the client. Search is only triggered when you use the search feature.

No PagePace account or cloud sync

PagePace does not provide account registration, sign-in, cloud sync of your library, or a PagePace-hosted backend for storing your reading history.

How we use information

On-device information is used only to provide app features, such as:

  • Tracking your reading goal and daily pace
  • Showing progress, schedules, and stats
  • Remembering your books and logs between sessions

Network requests for book search are used only to retrieve catalog suggestions you asked for.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

PagePace does not include third-party advertising SDKs, and does not use analytics services to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

We do not sell personal information.

Children

PagePace is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help you understand how local data can be removed (typically by deleting the app or clearing app data).

Data retention

Reading data remains on your device until you delete it, reset it in the app, or remove the app. We do not retain your reading library on PagePace servers because we do not host that data.

Your choices

  • Skip search: Enter book details manually; no catalog request is made.
  • Delete data: Use in-app reset (if available) or delete the app to remove local PagePace data from that device.
  • Device backups: Manage iCloud or other backups in your device/system settings.

International users

The app may contact Open Library’s servers when you search. Those servers may be located in different countries than you. By using search, you understand that your search query is processed by that third-party service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
